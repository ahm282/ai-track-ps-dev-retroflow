# 1. Team Membership and Identity

## How is a requester identified as a team member?

A requester is identified by their unique member ID.

**Decision:** Member ID is required for all operations requiring team membership validation.

---

## Should the request include a member name, and should the server verify that name is on the team?

**Decision:** Yes.

The request should contain the member ID. The server must verify that the member belongs to the target team.

---

## What counts as the same member?

**Decision:** Members are considered the same if they have the same unique ID.

Names are not used as unique identifiers.

---

## Are names case-sensitive?

**Decision:** No.

Name comparisons must be case-insensitive.

Examples:

- `Jonas`
- `jonas`
- `JONAS`

All represent the same name.

---

## Should surrounding whitespace be trimmed?

**Decision:** Yes.

Examples:

- `"Jonas"`
- `" Jonas "`
- `"  Jonas"`

All must be treated as `"Jonas"`.

---

## Can two members have the same name?

**Decision:** Yes.

Members are uniquely identified by their ID, not by their name.

Example:

```text
Member A:
ID: 123
Name: Jonas

Member B:
ID: 456
Name: Jonas
```

This is valid.

---

## What makes a valid team name or member name?

**Decision:**

- ASCII letters, ASCII digits and the space character only
- At least one letter or digit; leading and trailing spaces are allowed
- Pattern, identical on request DTOs and entities: `^ *[A-Za-z0-9]+( +[A-Za-z0-9]+)* *$`
- Values are stored exactly as submitted; the 255-character limit applies to the raw submitted value

Examples:

Valid:

```text
TeamA
Backend 01
Jonas 123
```

---

## Are blank strings invalid?

**Decision:** Yes.

Empty or blank values are not allowed.

Examples:

```text
""
" "
"     "
```

All are invalid.

---

## Are there maximum lengths or character restrictions?

**Decision:**

Maximum length:

```text
255 characters
```

Applies to:

- Team names
- Member names
- Retrospective titles

Feedback content and action-item content allow up to 4000 characters (see Length Restrictions).

---

## Does "a team must have at least one member" apply only at creation, or to future updates too?

**Decision:** It applies at all times.

A team must always contain at least one member.

Attempting to remove the last remaining member must fail.

---

## Must team names be unique?

**Decision:** Yes.

No two teams may share the same name.

Example:

```text
Team Alpha
```

can only exist once.

Names are compared trimmed and case-insensitively, so `team alpha ` is a duplicate of `Team Alpha`.

A duplicate fails with `400 Bad Request` and `{"message": "Team name already exists"}`, also when two requests race and the database rejects the second.

---

## Can members be added, removed, or renamed?

**Decision:** Yes.

The API supports:

- Add member
- Remove member
- Rename member

The team must still contain at least one member after any modification.

Removing a member is a soft delete: the member is marked inactive and the row is kept, so feedback they submitted keeps its submitter.

- Inactive members are not listed in team responses.
- Inactive members count as non-members for every membership check.
- Removing the last active member fails with `400 Bad Request` and `{"message": "A team must have at least one member"}`.

---

# 2. Retrospective Lifecycle and Creation

## What is the initial status?

**Decision:** OPEN by default.

A retrospective is created as:

```text
OPEN
```

unless another valid status is explicitly supplied.

---

## Which fields are required on creation?

### Required from client

```text
title
```

### Generated by server

```text
date = today
status = OPEN
```

### Optional overrides

```text
status
```

may be explicitly supplied during creation.

---

## What does the date represent?

**Decision:** The retrospective date.

The date is a calendar date only.

Examples:

```text
2026-10-26
```

No time or timezone information is stored.

---

## Can the date be in the future?

**Decision:** Yes.

The specification does not prohibit future dates.

---

## What happens when a team already has an open retrospective?

**Decision:**

Request fails.

Response:

```http
400 Bad Request
```

```json
{
  "message": "Only 1 retro can be open"
}
```

---

## What is the scope of "only one OPEN at a time"?

**Decision:** Per team.

Example:

```text
Team A -> 1 OPEN retrospective
Team B -> 1 OPEN retrospective
```

This is valid.

---

## Can a team create a new open retrospective after closing the previous one?

**Decision:** Yes.

Once the previous retrospective is closed, a new one may be created.

---

## Is closing an already closed retrospective allowed?

**Decision:** No.

Response:

```http
400 Bad Request
```

```json
{
  "message": "Retrospective already closed"
}
```

---

## Are retrospective titles and dates editable?

**Decision:** No.

The following fields are immutable:

- title
- date

---

## How does authority get checked when creating or closing?

**Decision:** Member ID validation.

The supplied member ID must belong to the team that owns the retrospective.

Over HTTP, `memberId` is a required field in the request body of create and close.

A member that is not on the team fails with `400 Bad Request` and `{"message": "Member is not part of this team"}`.

---

# 3. Feedback Operations and Rules

## Are feedback items editable and deletable?

**Decision:** Yes, while the retrospective is OPEN.

Allowed:

- create
- read
- update
- delete

Once a retrospective is CLOSED:

- update forbidden
- delete forbidden

---

## Which feedback fields are required?

### Required

```text
content
submitterId
type
```

### Validation

| Field | Rule |
|---------|---------|
| content | non-blank |
| content | max 4000 chars |
| submitterId | must belong to the team |
| type | valid enum |

The HTTP API identifies the submitter by `submitterId` only.

The service layer additionally accepts a submitter name, because the inherited business-rule tests call it that way. A name is resolved among the team's active members, trimmed and case-insensitively:

- No match fails with `400 Bad Request` and `{"message": "Member is not part of this team"}`.
- More than one match fails with `400 Bad Request` and `{"message": "Member name is ambiguous"}`.

---

## Must the submitter be a member of the retrospective's team?

**Decision:** Yes.

Non-members cannot submit feedback.

---

## Can a feedback item's type change after creation?

**Decision:** No.

Examples:

```text
POSITIVE -> ACTION_ITEM
NEGATIVE -> POSITIVE
```

Not allowed.

Type is immutable.

---

## Are feedback items retrievable only by retrospective?

**Decision:** Yes.

Endpoint:

```http
GET /retrospectives/{id}/feedback
```

No global feedback endpoint is required.

---

## Does closing freeze every kind of change?

**Decision:** Yes.

After closure all feedback becomes read-only.

Any change to feedback or action items in a closed retrospective fails with `400 Bad Request` and `{"message": "Retrospective is closed"}`.

---

# 4. Action Item Lifecycle and Representation

## How is an action item represented?

**Decision:** As a specialised feedback item.

Example:

```java
Feedback {
    UUID id;
    String content;
    FeedbackType type;

    Priority priority;
    Boolean completed;
}
```

---

## How are ACTION_ITEM field rules enforced?

### ACTION_ITEM

Must contain:

```text
priority
completed
```

### Non-action feedback

Must not contain:

```text
priority
completed
```

Invalid requests return `400 Bad Request` with:

```json
{
  "message": "Priority is required for action items"
}
```

or

```json
{
  "message": "Priority and completed are only allowed for action items"
}
```

---

## What are the initial values?

### Required

```text
priority
```

### Default

```text
completed = false
```

---

## Can action-item content or priority be edited?

**Decision:** Yes.

Requirements:

- Retrospective must be OPEN.
- Completed action items cannot become incomplete.

---

## Can an action item be marked complete after retrospective closure?

**Decision:** No.

Completion is considered a modification.

Closed retrospectives are immutable.

---

## Can completed action items be uncompleted?

**Decision:** No.

Allowed:

```text
false -> true
```

Not allowed:

```text
true -> false
```

Response:

```http
400 Bad Request
```

```json
{
  "message": "Action item cannot be uncompleted"
}
```

There is no dedicated uncomplete route. An uncomplete attempt is `PUT /api/feedback/{id}` with `completed: false` on a completed action item.

---

## Can action items be deleted while retrospective is open?

**Decision:** Yes.

Deletion is allowed while the retrospective remains OPEN.

---

## How should action items be filtered?

### Endpoint

```http
GET /action-items
```

### Filters

```http
GET /action-items?completed=true
GET /action-items?priority=HIGH
GET /action-items?priority=HIGH&completed=false
```

### Scope

All retrospectives.

### Ordering

1. OPEN retrospectives first
2. HIGH priority before MEDIUM before LOW
3. Creation order

### Pagination

Not required.

---

UUID.randomUUID()

## How should business-rule violations be handled?

**Decision:** Use:

```http

```

- Closing an already closed retrospective
- Non-member action
- Removing last team member

## Error Response Format

```json
{
  "message": "Only 1 retro can be open"
}
```

Examples:

```json
{
  "message": "Retrospective already closed"
}
```

```json
{
  "message": "Action item cannot be uncompleted"
}
```

Every error response uses this format, including errors raised outside the controllers. Stack traces are never returned.

| Situation | Status | Message |
|------------|---------|---------|
| Request validation failure | 400 | `field: message`, joined with `; ` |
| Unknown route | 404 | `No endpoint <METHOD> <path>` |
| Unsupported method | 405 | `Method <METHOD> is not supported for this endpoint` |
| Unsupported content type | 415 | `Content type must be application/json` |
| Unexpected server error | 500 | `Unexpected error` |

## What happens when a resource does not exist?

Response:

```http
404 Not Found
```

```json
{
  "message": "Retrospective not found"
}
```

Messages for other missing resources:

- `Team not found`
- `Member not found`
- `Feedback item not found`

---

## Are list endpoints paginated?

**Decision:** No.

Pagination is outside the assignment scope.

---

## Should responses include related objects?

**Decision:** No.

Example:

```http
GET /retrospectives/{id}
```

Returns retrospective data only.

Feedback must be loaded separately:

```http
GET /api/retrospectives/{id}/feedback
```

Members are part of a team, not a related object, so team responses include them.

| Resource | Fields |
|------------|---------|
| Team | `id`, `name`, `members` (active members only, each `id`, `name`) |
| Retrospective | `id`, `title`, `date`, `status`, `teamId` |
| Feedback and action item | `id`, `retrospectiveId`, `content`, `type`, `submitterId`, `submitterName`, `priority`, `completed` |

`priority` and `completed` are omitted from the response unless `type` is `ACTION_ITEM`.

# 6. Data and Operational Expectations

## Are duplicate retrospectives allowed?

**Decision:** Yes.

The only restriction is:

```text
One OPEN retrospective per team
```

Multiple closed retrospectives may share:

- title
- date

---

## Are duplicate feedback items allowed?

**Decision:** Yes.

Feedback content does not need to be unique.

---

## Should submitted names be preserved or normalised?

**Decision:**

Store values exactly as submitted.

For comparisons:

- trim whitespace
- compare case-insensitively

Example:

```text
"Jonas"
" jonas "
"JONAS"
```

All resolve to the same member.

---

## Is H2 in-memory storage sufficient?

**Decision:** Yes.

Suggested configuration:

```properties
spring.datasource.url=jdbc:h2:mem:retrodb
```

No persistence between restarts is required.

---

## Are audit or history features required?

**Decision:** No.

The system does not need to track:

- who created a retrospective
- who closed a retrospective
- when it was closed
- who completed an action item
- when it was completed
